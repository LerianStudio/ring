lerian · Act 04 — Strategic Discussion 01/04
/
Discussion 01
~12 min

Lerian Cloud — "Vercel for financial services."

Context
  • 70% of new logos enter directly through SaaS (vs. 100% BYOC 12 months ago).
  • Tenant-manager live since Q1/26. Clients deploy Lerian products and their own apps on Lerian infra — stickiness beyond traditional SaaS.
  • No competitor offers this capability today.
  • BYOC clients already requesting migration to managed tiers — organic upsell motion.
Questions for the board
  • 01
    Target profile & reference case
    Enterprise gravitates hybrid, mid-market accepts full SaaS. Which segment builds the reference case for the other?
  • 02
    GTM timing
    70% already choose SaaS. BYOC migrating up. Is this the signal to go-to-market with Cloud now — or do we need a larger proof base?
  • 03
    Pricing strategy
    Clients running their apps on Lerian infra creates platform lock-in. How do we price platform vs. product without cannibalizing either during transition?
